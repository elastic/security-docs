=== OpenAI ChatGPT Enterprise Resources Sharing Updated

Detects ChatGPT Enterprise audit events that publish a GPT or update sharing for GPTs, skills, or projects (`gpt_publish`, `update_sharing`, `skill_share`, `project_update_sharing`). Broadening share scope can expose custom instructions, tools, or proprietary agent configurations beyond the original audience.

==== Investigation guide



*Triage and analysis*



*Investigating OpenAI ChatGPT Enterprise Resources Sharing Updated*


A GPT was published or a GPT, skill, or project sharing setting changed. Broadening share scope can expose proprietary instructions, connectors, or data.

Escalate when `shared_to.recipient_type` or related fields indicate a wider audience than policy allows, the asset includes sensitive instructions or connectors, or the publisher is unexpected. Close as a false positive for intentional publication under an approved release process.


*Possible investigation steps*


- Identify the actor and the GPT, skill, or project identifiers on the alert.
- Review `openai_chatgpt_enterprise.audit_log.action_data.shared_to.recipient_type` and other share-scope fields when present.
- Confirm whether store or broad visibility was enabled and whether that matches change management.
- Look for related publish, share, or connector-link events from the same actor.


*False positive analysis*


- Teams intentionally publish GPTs or skills for approved internal or partner use under change management.


*Response and remediation*


- Restrict unauthorized shares, unpublish if needed, and inventory other published assets by the same actor.

*Setup*


This rule requires the OpenAI ChatGPT Enterprise integration to collect compliance logs into Elasticsearch.

==== Rule query


[source, js]
----------------------------------
from logs-openai_chatgpt_enterprise.audit_log-* metadata _id, _version, _index
| where
    data_stream.dataset == "openai_chatgpt_enterprise.audit_log" and
    event.action in ("gpt_publish", "update_sharing", "skill_share", "project_update_sharing")
| keep
    _id, _version, _index, @timestamp, event.action, user.id, user.email, source.ip,
    openai_chatgpt_enterprise.audit_log.action_data.gpt_id,
    openai_chatgpt_enterprise.audit_log.action_data.agent_id,
    openai_chatgpt_enterprise.audit_log.action_data.skill_id,
    openai_chatgpt_enterprise.audit_log.action_data.shared_to.recipient_type,
    openai_chatgpt_enterprise.audit_log.action_data.third_party_gizmo_id,
    openai_chatgpt_enterprise.audit_log.action_privilege, data_stream.*

----------------------------------
