By mapping out the open ports, attackers can gather critical information to plan and execute targeted attacks, gaining unauthorized access, compromising security, and potentially leading to data breaches, unauthorized control, or further exploitation of the targeted system. This rule defines a threshold-based approach to detect connection attempts from a single internal source to a wide range of destination ports on a single destination. | update | 18 

|<<prebuilt-rule-8-19-37-potential-low-packet-port-scan-detected, Potential Low-Packet Port Scan Detected>> | This rule identifies a potential low-packet port scan. It detects final flows or firewall connection attempts from one internal source to many destination ports on one destination and transport, while requiring no more than two packets in each matching observation. Attackers can use low-packet scans to identify exposed services while minimizing completed connections. Low packet count is a scan heuristic and does not by itself prove that the traffic contains bare TCP SYN packets. | update | 17
