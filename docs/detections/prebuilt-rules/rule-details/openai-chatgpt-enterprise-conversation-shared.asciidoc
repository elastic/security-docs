=== OpenAI ChatGPT Enterprise Conversation Shared

Detects ChatGPT Enterprise audit events where a conversation is shared. Sharing can expose prompts, completions, and attached files outside the original conversation participants and may be used to exfiltrate sensitive content.

==== Investigation guide



*Triage and analysis*



*Investigating OpenAI ChatGPT Enterprise Conversation Shared*


A conversation was shared. Sharing can expose prompts, responses, and attached context beyond the original participants.

Escalate when the audience is external or overly broad, the conversation contains sensitive data, or sharing coincides with downloads or mass deletion. Close as a false positive for expected collaboration inside the organization.


*Possible investigation steps*


- Identify the user, conversation, and share-related fields on the alert.
- Determine the intended audience and whether it matches workspace sharing policy.
- Review nearby message content, file downloads, or additional shares from the same user.
- Check whether the conversation includes credentials, proprietary code, or customer data.


*False positive analysis*


- Users intentionally share conversations for collaboration within the organization when that sharing path is approved.


*Response and remediation*


- Revoke unauthorized shares and review other sharing activity by the same actor.
- If sensitive content was exposed, follow your data-incident process for the recipients and channel used.


==== Setup



*Setup*


This rule requires the OpenAI ChatGPT Enterprise integration to collect compliance logs into Elasticsearch.

==== Rule query


[source, js]
----------------------------------
from logs-openai_chatgpt_enterprise.audit_log-* metadata _id, _version, _index
| where
    data_stream.dataset == "openai_chatgpt_enterprise.audit_log" and
    event.action == "conversation_share"
| keep
    _id, _version, _index, @timestamp, event.action, user.id, user.email, source.ip,
    openai_chatgpt_enterprise.audit_log.action_data.conversation_id,
    openai_chatgpt_enterprise.audit_log.action_data.shared_conversation_id,
    openai_chatgpt_enterprise.audit_log.action_data.already_exists,
    openai_chatgpt_enterprise.audit_log.action_privilege, data_stream.*

----------------------------------
